# PGS — Editorial guidance and production routing

**Source:** Curated publication-safe rules from `PGS_Content_OS_Living_Project_Master_v1.0.md` (2026-10-16) and approved PGS content decisions. **Not** a copy of the PGS technology/portal project status or connected service setup.

## Mission
Guide a student or parent through a specific education or career decision, including what they want to become, why, feasible choices, costs, risks, qualification or licensing rules, and the best next question. Individual fit matters: avoid generic 'the best country' or guaranteed admission/jobs.

## Mandatory analysis before content
- Create, reverse-engineer and **validate** persona, decision stage, academic profile and constraints; do not treat assumed student archetypes as fact.
- Explore aspirations, status/belonging, family financial pressure, mobility, safety, peer norms and information sources without assuming one universal motive.
- Compare **pros/cons**, alternatives, **before/after**, realistic costs, eligibility, career path and opportunity limits.
- Mentor stance: what works for one student may not work for another. Say **good fit for / think twice if / consider instead** where useful.
- For medical/dental topics separately verify exam authority, country rules, training path, registration, licensing and current effective dates; passing an exam does not guarantee a job.
- Quote official primary sources for deadlines, scholarships, rules, country visa/work policies and licensing. Verify publication/effective dates. No invented student cases.

## Core script process
Persona creation → persona validation → topic/familiar belief → question/gap/contrast → what/why/how → realistic case or comparison → initial proof → counterevidence → deeper analysis → balanced takeaway → free educational resource or relevant mentor CTA → update rule.

## Locked schedule
- **Two original full-length YouTube flagship videos/week**, initially general/nonmedical and medical/dental mix.
- Repurpose clips **after recording** and human selection. Record context/citations so clips stand alone.
- Add independent original VO Reels as capacity permits.
- Seven opportunity inserts: **Scholarship Radar, PhD/Research Radar, Internship Radar, Deadline Watch, Medical Pathway Watch, Profile Builder, University Face-Off**.
- Breaking news only for meaningful verified change; opportunity slots are *optional*, not fake promises or extra compulsory full-length videos.
- One recurring topic bank of **210 candidates** is imported unchanged. Its 36-week proposed runway is planning, not approved scripts or verified job opportunities.

## Topic families
General study/careers; MBBS/MDS and licensing; PhD/research funding; internships/profile building; live official opportunities; student questions, fears and myths; AI and the real value of degrees; cost, debt and job outcomes; family/identity choices.

## What to avoid
Generic rankings, unsupported 'students get X salary', copied competitor content, fearbait, visa certainty, fabricated statistics, forced sales pitches, declaring 210 ideas published, or auto-generating clips without creative review.

**Editorial documents are not infrastructure status:** Airtable, R2, APIs and production software are handled in their own private operations records. This public repo intentionally does not reproduce private access details or system identifiers.
