**Editorial proposition:** Education journalism inspired by the investigative, evidence-led approach of technology publications (not imitation of their wording or articles). Track people, incentives, what changed, trade-offs, surprising consequences, and practical decisions. Students first, parent lens when relevant. Preserve existing 10-beat PGS script block template.

**Audience navigation (V0.5):** The 30 new HAY VO ideas are grouped into **five topic blocks** in the appendix: Medical/Dental · Broad Students · MBA/MiM/Companies · AI/Research · Government Rules/Exams. Episode IDs are retained for cross-referencing the existing resource companion and schedules.

**Production LOCK:** 2 flagship YouTube videos/week; clips extracted after recording; additional original VO Reels; supplemental recurring series inserts; breaking news only when warranted. The inventories below are **topic candidates**, not a commitment to publish all of them. Long-form general and medical episodes compete for the two weekly flagship slots; no added two-a-week medical obligation.

**Workflow LOCK:** 1. Investigate and select topic; 2. Develop and QC headline/angle; 3. Design genuinely useful resource/CTA; 4. Research and draft body in existing PGS script blocks; 5. Editorial and factual QC; 6. Publish and track response. Information from competitors is read-only inspiration, never copied.

**Research discipline:** No invented student case, fabricated numbers, visa promises, employer claims or program rules. For country jobs compare occupation + geography + work authorization + language + costs + time horizon, not an unsupported overall 'best'. For medical licensing/current policy check official bodies and date-stamp. Review consent before using identifiable student stories.

## NEW — Phased Content Production Roadmap V0.3 (planning layer, 14 October 2026)

> **Intent:** Pre-plan enough of the PGS editorial queue for months so the creator can concentrate on research, scripts, recording, and publishing instead of reopening topic selection every week. The original G/M/R/I/X topic inventories remain intact below. This roadmap **organizes** them; it does not commission all 180 slots or rewrite the existing PGS script template.

### What is locked vs what is flexible

**Locked:** Two original long-form YouTube videos each week (Tuesday / Friday as proposed publishing days); 2–3 relevant clips *per completed flagship*; 1 sustainable independent VO Reel each week (increase to 2 only when ready); up to 1 relevant opportunity insert each week, only if verified; additional breaking news only on meaningful change. Publish short vertical video to both YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, adapted for each platform. The separate intro video is **outside** these two weekly slots.

**Locked editorial strategy:** 50% optimistic/opportunity-oriented, 40% investigation/decision analysis, ~10% direct PGS offers over the **overall rolling published mix**. Educational posts may feature a fitting **free** resource CTA without counting as direct sales. The flagship queue below deliberately alternates 6 opportunity / 6 investigation topics per phase; use the independent VO/inserts and occasional direct-offer posts to calibrate the full mix over time. Do not sacrifice an important educational story to make a percentage fit one week.

**Flexible:** Exact dates, headline wording, VO selection, insert choice, resource details, clip selection and the Phase 2–6 running order. The first few episodes focus on **current student conversations** (AI, jobs abroad, why leave, achievable profile growth); they are *not assertions about verified October 2026 news*. Fresh/current claims must be separately researched before release.

### Simple predictable weekly release pattern

| Day | Primary slot | Secondary slot / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Independent VO Reel / Short | Student question, current context or related standalone story; optional community poll |
| Tuesday | **YouTube flagship A** | Supporting source/resource link ready; separate introduction video excluded |
| Wednesday | Clip A1 | One verified insert **only when worthwhile**; otherwise no extra post |
| Thursday | Clip A2 | Editorial research and next upload preparation |
| Friday | **YouTube flagship B** | Supporting source/resource link ready |
| Saturday | Clip B1 | Community questions/comments can inform next VO |
| Sunday | Clip B2 | Review performance; queue next week; optional inserted episode *instead of*, not always on top of, a short slot |

**Clip rule:** Plan clipable questions while writing but select actual clips **after recording**. A clip may need a new first sentence, on-screen context and citations to stand alone. Extra clips are placed in a buffer; 2 is the minimum, 3 only if valuable. For Week 1 there may be no previous-video clips yet—do not pad the calendar.

**Production buffer:** Ideally keep 2 flagship videos + 4 clips ready in advance; work in a two-week batch. A small team can protect the same weekly rhythm even if a breaking story is inserted. No auto-posting solely because a scheduler has a slot.

### How inserts work across every phase

The seven recurring series (Scholarship Radar, PhD & Research Radar, Internship Radar, Deadline Watch, Medical Pathway Watch, Profile Builder, University Face-Off) are **supplements**, not the headline editorial calendar. Research them at their prescribed cadence in the I01–I30 inventory. Each week an editor may choose **zero or one** strong, verifiable insert. The I-code alongside a week below is a *research prompt*, never an assertion that an opportunity is currently open. Prefer an official primary source with checked deadline, eligibility, program dates, country, intake and last-verified timestamp. If none is substantive, publish no insert. Policy-related breaking news is outside series cadence and only published when needed.

### Resource-library and commercial link (phase planning, *not* final CTA pass)

- Each flagship should end in a useful, relevant next step: source pack, spreadsheet, checklist, medical pathway references, student/parent decision aid, opportunity tracker or community discussion. **Actual CTA wording and the deliverable are decided only after headline and research approval**. Publishing a free guide must not depend on a purchase.
- Direct PGS product content (approximately 10% of combined posts over time) can demonstrate the Guided Toolkit, mentor/kanban planning, transparent partner-university admission help, or selective-admissions advising. Do not pretend PGS can guarantee visas, placements or admission. Route students to services based on fit, not force every video into a sales pitch.
- Label and date all source-backed resources. Free starter kit is a default broad resource; specialist medical, application, scholarship and financial worksheets are more useful when they directly match the episode.

### Workflow for low-stress execution: approval gates + episode tracker

| Stage | Definition of done | Owner / next action |
|---|---|---|
| **Idea reserved** | G/M ID placed in one phase; persona and optimistic/investigative lens tagged | Editorial planning |
| **Research brief** | Primary sources, contradictory evidence, 'bigger problem', scope and timeliness checked | Research/QC |
| **Headline approved** | Three editorial alternatives considered; one chosen; no unsupported claims | Creator review |
| **Resource approved** | A real useful resource exists or a no-gate public source page is linked | Resource editor |
| **Script approved** | Existing PGS ten-beat blocks; pros/cons, before/after, mentor lens, nuanced conclusion | Creator/QC |
| **Recorded** | Full video captured; proposed clip timestamps noted | Production |
| **Edited / verified** | Fact-check, captions, audio, title, thumbnail, appropriate disclaimer | Editor/QC |
| **Scheduled / published** | Links to related resources; clips queued only after final master | Publishing |
| **Learned / updated** | Topic, source links, updates, questions, qualified leads, clip performance recorded | Weekly review |

**Work-in-progress limit:** Work actively on at most two long-form scripts at once, plus one original VO and one optional insert. Keep the later six-phase queue visible but do not expand every entry into scripts now. Maintain a small **breaking-news reserve** and a replacement list for stale opportunities.

**Research cadence and freshness:** Weekly check the seven insert categories; daily lightweight headline triage only if capacity allows. Publish timely news only if verified and genuinely useful to one of the five student personas or parent layer. Legal, visa, financial and medical claims require date-specific reputable primary sources and appropriate expert review.

### Acceptance checkpoints and change control

- **NOW:** QC Phase 1 **episode topics and ordering only**, then develop the first two briefs. Do not prematurely finalize CTA assets, headlines or scripts for all 72.
- **After Weeks 2, 4 and 6:** Review evidence, response, resource requests and operational effort; adjust later topics while preserving the 2/week schedule.
- **Four weeks before each next phase:** Revisit all 12 candidates, replace outdated ones, refresh optimistic/critical balance, identify sources and choose the first two to script.
- **Never delete G/M/R/I/X IDs when changing sequence:** Keep the original inventory as the research source of truth. Add statuses, replacement references and version history rather than silently renumbering.
