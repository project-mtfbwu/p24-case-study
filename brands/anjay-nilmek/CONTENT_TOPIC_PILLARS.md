## Two complementary publishing tracks
Shorts/Reels for focused news, hook, one useful insight, personal field observation; long-form YouTube for deep, source-backed explainers, experiments and building stories. Some short clips may come from long-form, but do not require every short to be repurposed.

## Writing workflow for owner-submitted scripts
1. Preserve the author's raw script and all important ideas.
2. Analyze reference script/hook/psychology only if labeled as a reference.
3. Present **exactly THREE** different story overviews, not the full script.
4. Owner chooses one or a hybrid; develop blocks/scene rhythm/persona and evidence.
5. Record approved techniques as P-001, P-002 etc; no technique becomes canon without approval.
6. Maintain version history; shoot/production follows script approval.
