# Sourcebyjay — Later production text and continuity directions

**Status:** Owner-specified editorial corrections made after initial case-study master, October 12–13, 2026. Specific current output instruction supersedes V1.3's automatic visual/rendering recommendation, while the V1.3 research and QC stages remain active.

## Mandatory slide handoff
Stage 3 **produces text-only Markdown/document by default**, not slide art, Photoshop files, ZIPs, or mockups. The design is applied separately when requested. Every published slide's visible content has **exactly three** fields:

- **Headline** — company-specific strong main headline; meaningful curiosity, not generic hero worship.
- **Description** — the explanatory story, concise, evidence-led, with narrative connection.
- **Data** — verified figures, proof, sources, or appropriately labeled qualitative evidence. Never fabricate statistics for the sake of filling space.

Behind-the-scenes registers (sources, confidence, decision, QC, change notes) stay in research documents and do not become a fourth visible slide field.

## Narrative continuity (mandatory)
Treat the carousel like a musical arrangement, not 14 isolated mini-posters:
- Each slide answers an issue raised by the last and creates a natural need for the next.
- Rhythm varies: hook → setup → context → reveal → tension → consequences → payoff → actionable application.
- One new meaningful idea per slide; do not repeat empty 'but here's the twist' lines.
- A powerful but truthful magazine-style curiosity gap may appear in secondary hooks/captions. Avoid clickbait that fails to pay off.
- Avoid generic AI voice, fake drama, unsupported giant comparisons, and random extra statistics.

## Current naming
- Use **Data** instead of 'Transition to Data' for evidence.
- Label actionable learning **Takeaways** ('what we can learn'). **Secret Sauce** is an evidence-backed business insight and can remain a content concept; do not rename every section Sauce or Takeaways just because they sound catchy.
- The original V1.3 14-slide research spine stays authoritative unless the owner approves a merged founder slide for a specific company.
- The company name remains the primary/visually dominant Slide 01 subject; secondary cover copy can create a gossip-magazine-style *real* curiosity gap.
- Final output is a single Markdown text master unless a different format is explicitly requested.

## Latest-vs-old rule
Original V1.3 says Stage 3 may render slides. That describes a capability, **not current standing permission**. This addendum controls today's default handoff.
