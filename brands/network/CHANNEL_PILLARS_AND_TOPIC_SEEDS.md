# Fan Pages — channel architecture and topic seeds

**Status:** Editorial planning inventory, October 2026; not evidence that every channel has launched or that every topic is scripted. Share research thinking, not one identical story across brands.

| Lane | Promise | Topic starters and formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company Case Studies / Sourcebyjay | Reverse-engineer actual company decisions | 25-company Batch 01; money, founders, investors, sales, competitors, Secret Sauce, turning points, takeaways | Active master |
| AI / Tech — What Happened & Why | What occurred, why it matters to people | Verified AI releases, automation of tasks, privacy, work, historical tech parallels, quality-of-life effects, pros/cons | Locked AI Revolution 12-topic framework; news stories need source checks |
| KPI / Mini-MBA | Business metrics people can actually use | Revenue vs profit vs cash flow; margin vs markup; unit economics; customer acquisition cost; payback; retention; inventory turns; contribution margin; working capital; AOV; break-even | Proposed topic seeds |
| Future Jobs / Career 2045+ | Real jobs now; plausible work choices in 5, 10 and 25–30 years | Job Radar, hiring news, AI task exposure, skills/portfolio, internships, alternate career paths, cost and risk | Template v1.1 proposed |
| Psychology / Motivation | Real human patterns rather than empty inspiration | Persuasion, self-concept, status, belonging, habits, why people follow stories, effort vs strategy, choice and uncertainty | Planning lane |
| Made in India / Inside Manufacturing | How things are built and sold | Factory process, component supply chains, production costs, sourcing, Indian MSMEs, quality testing, distribution | Later expansion candidate |

## Network-level distribution plans (not implemented)
Editorial-first retro-magazine visual identity; carousels/images first, short contextual video clips when useful. A possible production chain: sourced topic → verified structured content → JSON/PSD automation → human visual QC → scheduling → measure results. **This repo does not claim an automated publishing system exists.**

## Topic selection check
Who cares? Why today? What's surprising? What sources exist? What behavior or decision can the reader improve? Would this work better for PGS, Sourcebyjay, Careers, AI Revolution, or Nilmek? Adapt angle to reader, not copy/paste paragraphs.
