## Five pathways a person may value
1. **Maintain** present existence or stability.
2. **Improve** the quality or reliability of existing life.
3. **Expand** opportunities, skills, choices or experience.
4. **Enjoy** life, entertainment, comfort, play and delight.
5. **Express** identity, beliefs, interests and community.

These describe useful possible values, not proven laws or exhaustive psychological diagnoses. People also make inconsistent choices, enjoy art for its own sake, or act for others.

## Four questions
**Existence:** what life or situation am I in? **Stability:** can I maintain it? **Happiness:** do I enjoy it? **Aspiration:** what might I change or keep?

**BODY / MIND / SELF / TRIBE / WORLD** are simultaneous context influences, not sequential stages.

## Application to media and selling
A story lets someone imagine experiences and patterns they have not lived directly. Start with the persona and their own context, connect a concrete event to a realistic concern or desire, explain why it works, test other interpretations, and give an honest, achievable action. Real value and ethical persuasion matter more than a manufactured emotional trigger.
