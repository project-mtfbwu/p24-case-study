# Shared five-process editorial framework

**Authority:** User-accepted repeatable cross-brand method (October 11, 2026); this is a **process**, not one universal slide/video template.

1. **Persona creation by reverse engineering:** Identify person's situation, environment, life stage, goals, values, interests, fear, social context, information sources, skills, constraints, and the real decision they face.
2. **Persona validation:** Challenge guesses with conversations, audience comments, credible research, search/job market evidence and observed behavior. Label archetypes as hypotheses until validated.
3. **Story/video beats:** Topic → familiar belief → curiosity gap → contrast → what / why / how → relatable real case → initial validation → contradictory evidence → deeper verification → evidence-backed conclusion → upgraded perspective. Select and compress the beats appropriate to format; don't mechanically force all into every short.
4. **Evidence and judgment:** Pros **and** cons, alternatives, before/after, source dates, real-world applicability, rival explanations; a thoughtful mentor/advisor point of view, not universal instructions.
5. **Editing and delivery:** Choose pacing and scene blocks, build smooth transitions, keep only meaningful new ideas, match hook to payoff, proof-check visuals and numbers, human QC before publishing, and update when real conditions change.

**Cross-brand lesson:** Common psychology and evidence logic, **different audience promise and visible fields**. Sourcebyjay company cases = 14-slide master; AI Revolution = flexible 12-topic library; Careers = proposed 10-beat system; Anjay Nilmek = his own persona; PGS = student decision support. Never merge them into one generic carousel.

## Storytelling quality checks
- Hook relevance and honest curiosity, not fabricated mystery.
- Every transition answers the previous question or creates a necessary next one.
- Build → tension → evidence → reveal → consequence → practical payoff, with variable pacing.
- At least one real-world analogy, case, or observed system when warranted.
- Source facts separate from interpretation, forecasts and illustrative examples.
- Pros/cons and before/after need comparable evidence rather than decorative labels.
- Show **what we can learn**; avoid generic 'dream big' endings.
