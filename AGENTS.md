# Agent instructions — P24 Editorial OS

Read `MEMORY_INDEX.md` and `PROJECT_STATUS.md` before changing editorial files. Then read the exact **brand master** needed for this task. This repository is a durable editorial memory, not permission to generate or publish everything.

## Authority (highest to lowest)
1. Creator's explicit, dated approval of a specific asset/stage or a later correction.
2. Current approved brand master, including addenda that record later changes.
3. Approved shared editorial principles and evidence rules.
4. Working drafts and proposed templates.
5. Superseded archives, examples, and ideas — context only.

A later *specific* approved correction supersedes a conflicting older clause without silently rewriting the historical master. Record that exception in `operations/DECISIONS.md`. Never promote an unapproved draft to canon.

## Hard stops
- **Sourcebyjay case studies:** Stage 1 research → QC 1 → Stage 2 14-slide script → QC 2 → Stage 3 **text-only production handoff by default** → QC 3. Publishing requires separate instruction. The active V1.3 master is preserved; read its later production-output addendum.
- **Sourcebyjay slides:** `Headline`, `Description`, `Data` — exactly three visible fields. Strong narrative continuity. No render/PSD/Figma design unless the creator explicitly requests it.
- **AI Revolution:** `Promise`, `Story`, `Aha!` is a separate format; its 12 topics are a library, not a compulsory 12-slide layout.
- **PGS:** persona first, then mentor judgment and source checks; two original flagship YouTube videos per week, optional researched inserts and human-selected clips. Do not invent admissions outcomes or official opportunities.
- **Future Careers:** v1.1 is **PROPOSED**, not locked. Don't invent live vacancies or label 2050s forecasts as certainty.
- **Anjay Nilmek:** Observer + Explorer + Builder provisional persona. For an owner-written raw script, offer exactly three story overviews before drafting the selected full script.
- No agent may create a verified quote, source, financial metric, live job posting, approval, or real-world experiment from inference alone.
- Keep user/company/students' private data, connected-service IDs, credentials and unpublished client material out of this **public** repository.
- Do not silently rewrite approved docs. Create dated amendments or change logs, and keep rejected alternatives tagged `REJECTED` or `SUPERSEDED`.

## At start of a session
Report: (1) applicable brand and active canon (2) approved vs proposed vs superseded (3) relevant topic/story state (4) next permitted action. **Do not advance an editorial gate on your own.**

## When updating
Add a source link, date, authority/status label and change reason. For each story maintain one `status.md` pointing to evidence and approved script passes. On editorial disagreement, show both readings rather than inventing creator approval. American English; clear, human, magazine-style writing, with humor only when appropriate.
