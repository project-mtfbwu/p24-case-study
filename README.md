# P24 Fan Pages — Editorial Operating System

**Starter repository · 2026-10-17**

Shared editorial memory for business case studies, education guidance, AI/technology explainers, future careers, and creator storytelling. **Human approval is the final gate.**

## Start here
1. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [MEMORY_INDEX.md](MEMORY_INDEX.md).
2. Check [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md) for what is locked, working, superseded, or not researched.
3. Load the relevant brand canon; do **not** apply another brand's slide structure automatically.
4. Preserve decisions, evidence, and approval gates. Never invent figures, quotations, job openings, or permissions.

- `canon/`: shared persona, psychology, storytelling, and quality rules.
- `brands/sourcebyjay/`: 14-slide V1.3 current master, company discovery, original 25-company registry.

## Important
This repository is **public** as of 2026-10-17. Do not commit confidential client data, student information, credentials, private financial plans, unpublished business access details, or connected-service identifiers. The content here is an editorial starter, not evidence that each company story or external job claim has been verified.

**Ownership and editorial approval:** The creator decides which research, script, slide copy, design, and publication output becomes approved.
