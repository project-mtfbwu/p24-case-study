# Evidence / causality rules shared across pages

1. Quote verifiable first-party filings, official announcements, regulator/university documents, historical press, and independent reporting with working source links and dates. A company's marketing claim is not independent verification.
2. Preserve what was knowable **at the decision date**. Don't import hindsight to make a founder seem clairvoyant.
3. Do not confuse revenue, bookings, valuation, profit, net margin, cash flow, fundraising, debt and secondary share buying.
4. Compare similar metrics, countries, dates and denominators; flag incomparable baselines.
5. Explain counterevidence and rival causes; observed growth after an event does not establish the event caused growth.
6. Keep `FACT / COMPANY CLAIM / INDEPENDENT REPORT / INFERENCE / SCENARIO / UNKNOWN` distinct.
7. Medical/immigration/scholarship/licensing/job openings require official current date/eligibility links. Expired opportunities must never masquerade as open.
8. Direct quotations need attributable original context. Celebrity/politician/founder quotes don't become true because they sound memorable.
9. Images, charts and captions must carry the same factual restraint as the text. AI-made photos are not documentary evidence.
10. Never claim editor peer review or publishing approval that did not actually happen.
